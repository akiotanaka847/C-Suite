# Halcyon Motors — Company Overview

**Halcyon Motors** is a US-based electric-vehicle startup founded in 2019, now at
Series C with roughly 480 employees and about $140M in trailing vehicle revenue.
Our single product today is the **Halcyon C1**, an affordable electric crossover
starting at **$36,900**.

## The thesis

Mainstream American buyers are not waiting for a faster or fancier EV. They are
waiting for one they can actually afford, that charges easily outside big cities,
and that won't surprise them with service costs. Every incumbent EV that targets
this buyer — the household trading in a 12-year-old gas crossover — still tends to
drift upmarket on price and trim complexity. Halcyon's bet is to **hold the
affordable price point as the product**, not as a temporary discount.

## What makes the C1 different

- **Single, option-light trim.** One configuration with a short options list keeps
  build complexity and price down, and speeds the production ramp.
- **NACS-native from launch.** The C1 ships with a NACS port, so buyers get Tesla
  Supercharger access on day one — directly addressing the charging anxiety that
  dominates first-time-EV-buyer hesitation.
- **LFP battery chemistry.** Chosen for cost and cycle life over peak range,
  matching a value buyer who cares about longevity and total cost of ownership.
- **Direct-to-consumer + mobile service.** Avoids the legacy dealer margin stack
  and brings service to suburban/exurban customers.

## Where we are

- Production is ramping from ~2,600 to a target of **4,000 units/month**.
- Order backlog sits at ~**31,000 units**.
- Gross margin per vehicle is currently **negative (~-6%)**; reaching
  gross-margin-positive before the next raise is the year's north-star metric.
- Runway is roughly **14 months** at ~$18M/month burn.

## The competitive frame

We do not try to beat Tesla on 0–60 or Lucid on range. We win on **total cost of
ownership** and **charging access** against the Tesla Model Y, Chevy Equinox EV,
Hyundai Ioniq 5 / Kia EV6, and Ford Mustang Mach-E. The looming structural threat
is **BYD** — currently tariff-gated out of the US, but the global cost leader that
sets the long-run price ceiling for the whole segment.

## Why external monitoring matters here

This is a sector where the news *is* the strategy. A Tesla price cut, an IRA
Section 30D tax-credit rule change, a new tariff on Chinese cells, a competitor
recall, or a battery-supplier disruption can each change the buy decision for our
customer within a week. Halcyon tracks these signals continuously and routes them
to the department head who owns the response.
