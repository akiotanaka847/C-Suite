# Halcyon Motors — Regulatory & Legal

Owner: **Taylor Brooks (Head of Strategy & Customer, also Head of Legal & Regulatory)**

In EVs, regulation and policy move the buy decision as much as product does. Three
areas dominate Halcyon's legal/regulatory watch.

## 1. EV tax credit (IRA Section 30D)
The federal **$7,500 clean-vehicle credit** materially changes the effective price
to our value buyer. Our exposure runs through the **critical-minerals and battery-
component sourcing requirements** — which is exactly why single-sourcing LFP cells
from CATL is a *legal* risk as well as a supply risk.

- **Watch:** Treasury/IRS guidance on 30D eligibility, foreign-entity-of-concern
  (FEOC) rules, MSRP caps, and income caps.
- **Impact:** A change in eligibility flips our effective price overnight and forces
  same-day updates to on-site pricing math (Marketing) and reservation economics
  (Finance).

## 2. Tariffs and trade policy
- **Watch:** US tariffs on Chinese EVs, cells, and components; Section 301 actions;
  any change to the BYD import posture.
- **Impact:** Tariffs on imported cells raise our BOM cost directly. A change in the
  Chinese-EV import posture also moves the long-run price ceiling for the whole
  affordable-EV segment (the BYD threat).

## 3. Safety and recalls (NHTSA)
- **Watch:** NHTSA recalls and investigations — both any touching Halcyon and those
  touching **shared suppliers or components** (e.g., a cell or component recall that
  also affects our bill of materials).
- **Impact:** Safety signals are high-severity by default. A recall touching a shared
  component escalates to the principal same-day.

## Escalation posture
Legal/Regulatory operates at **escalate** authority: material legal or regulatory
risk reaches the principal (Jordan) the same day rather than being auto-actioned.
Contract matters (supplier, financing) and compliance findings route through Legal
for review before any commitment.
