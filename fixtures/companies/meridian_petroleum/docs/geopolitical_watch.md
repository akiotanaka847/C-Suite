# Meridian Petroleum — Geopolitical Watch & Iran-Conflict Exposure

This is the document that explains *why* Meridian runs a continuous external scan.
For an independent refiner, geopolitics is not background news — it is the single
largest driver of refining margin and supply reliability quarter to quarter.

## The Iran conflict is the dominant risk right now

The ongoing **Israel–Iran conflict** sits directly upstream of our P&L through
several channels, any one of which can move margin within a day:

1. **Crude price.** Strikes on Iranian energy infrastructure (Kharg Island, refineries)
   or escalation fears put a war premium on **Brent**, which spikes our feedstock cost
   and compresses refining margin until we can pass it through.
2. **Strait of Hormuz shipping.** Roughly a fifth of the world's oil moves through
   Hormuz. Threats to the strait spike **VLCC tanker rates** and **war-risk insurance
   premiums**, can force cargo **diversions**, and threaten the **38%** of our slate
   that depends on Hormuz-routed barrels.
3. **Sanctions.** Escalation tightens **OFAC** enforcement on **Iranian crude** moving
   through Fujairah and ship-to-ship transfers — making some cheap barrels legally
   radioactive and putting due-diligence pressure on our cargo book.
4. **OPEC+ response.** How **OPEC+** (and **Saudi Aramco**'s spare capacity) responds
   to a supply shock determines whether a spike is brief or structural.

## What we monitor continuously

- **Conflict escalation** — strikes on energy infrastructure, terminal/refinery damage,
  shipping-lane threats in the Strait of Hormuz and the Bab-el-Mandeb / Red Sea.
- **Crude prices** — Brent and WTI levels, the Brent–WTI spread, and the term structure.
- **OPEC+** — output decisions, quota compliance, and Saudi spare-capacity signals.
- **Sanctions** — OFAC designations, enforcement actions, and Iranian-crude flows.
- **Shipping** — VLCC day rates, war-risk insurance premiums, and tanker diversions
  (watch operators like Frontline).
- **Inventories & demand** — weekly EIA and monthly IEA / OPEC reports.
- **Refiner peers** — crack-spread and margin commentary from Valero, Marathon
  Petroleum, Phillips 66, and PBF Energy.

## How signals route inside Meridian

| Signal | Owner | Typical response |
|--------|-------|------------------|
| Crude spike / margin impact | Strategy + Commercial desk | Hedge laddering, slate optimization, scenario brief to the principal |
| Strait of Hormuz / shipping disruption | Operations | Cargo rerouting, feedstock diversification, insurance review |
| OFAC / Iranian-crude sanctions | Legal & Compliance | Enhanced cargo screening, hold lifting until cleared |
| Hedge / VaR / margin-call exposure | Finance | Hedge roll, treasury margin, VaR trim |
| Pump-price politics | Marketing | Lagged pass-through, transparent pricing statement, crisis comms |

The standing rule: any **unhedged crude exposure above the desk limit**, any
**refinery safety/environmental signal**, and any **material sanctions exposure**
escalate to the principal the **same day**.
