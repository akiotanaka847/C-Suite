# Meridian Petroleum — Company Overview

**Meridian Petroleum** is a US Gulf Coast independent founded in 2008, now
PE-backed with roughly **1,400 employees** and about **$3.2B** in annual revenue.
We do three things: we **refine** crude into fuels at a 185,000 barrel-per-day
Gulf Coast refinery, we **trade** crude and refined products on a disciplined
desk, and we **market** fuels to wholesale, retail, and commercial buyers.

## The thesis

A mid-size independent refiner lives and dies on two things it does not control:
the **price of crude** and the **reliability of supply**. Both are set, right now,
by events thousands of miles away — the Israel–Iran conflict, OPEC+ output
decisions, and whether tankers can move safely through the **Strait of Hormuz**.
Meridian's bet is that the refiner who **hedges the price risk and diversifies the
feedstock before the shock arrives** keeps margin and supply intact through the
disruptions that take less-prepared competitors offline.

## What makes Meridian different

- **Flexible crude slate.** The refinery can swing between sour Middle East crude
  and lighter WTI / West African barrels, so we are not hostage to one shipping lane.
- **Disciplined hedging book.** We take price risk on purpose, within desk limits,
  never by accident — collars and swaps cap catastrophic downside on margin.
- **Conservative sanctions posture.** Every Middle East-origin cargo is screened
  against OFAC and Iranian-crude sanctions before it lifts. A clean cargo beats a
  cheap cargo, every time.
- **Real-time geopolitical intelligence.** The trading desk and Strategy track the
  conflict, OPEC+, freight rates, and inventory reports continuously, because the
  news moves our margin within hours.

## Where we are

- Refining capacity is **185,000 bpd**; current throughput is ~**171,000 bpd**.
- Refining margin per barrel is **~$14.20** against an **$18.00** target.
- Q3 crude exposure is **45% hedged** against a **70%** target.
- **38%** of the feedstock slate still runs through the **Strait of Hormuz** — the
  number we are working to cut below 25%.
- Trading-desk value-at-risk sits at **$9.5M** against a **$10M** board limit.

## Why external monitoring matters here

In this business, the news *is* the strategy. A strike on an Iranian or Saudi
loading terminal, an OPEC+ output surprise, a fresh OFAC designation, a spike in
Strait of Hormuz war-risk insurance, or an EIA inventory miss can each move our
refining margin and our supply within a single day. Meridian tracks these signals
continuously and routes each one to the department head who owns the response —
geopolitics and margin to Strategy and the desk, shipping to Operations, sanctions
to Compliance, and price risk to Finance.
