# Meridian Petroleum — Regulatory & Sanctions Compliance

For a refiner that buys Middle East crude and trades cargoes globally, compliance is
not paperwork — it is the difference between a clean business and an existential
legal and reputational risk. The governing principle: **a clean cargo beats a cheap
cargo, every time.**

## OFAC / Iranian-crude sanctions

The live exposure is **OFAC enforcement on Iranian crude**. As the Iran conflict
escalates, the US has tightened designations on the intermediaries, vessels, and
ship-to-ship transfer networks that move Iranian barrels — frequently staged through
**Fujairah** and disguised origins.

**Our controls:**

- **Mandatory pre-lift screening** on every Middle East-origin cargo against OFAC SDN
  and vessel lists.
- **Enhanced due diligence** on cargoes loaded at or near known transshipment hubs
  (two Fujairah-loaded cargoes are currently under EDD).
- **Hold-until-cleared**: no cargo lifts until Compliance signs off. No exceptions for
  a cheap barrel.
- **Counterparty KYC** kept current on every trading and charter counterparty.

## Environmental & fuels regulation

- **EPA Renewable Fuel Standard (RFS)** — RIN obligations and compliance on the fuels
  we produce.
- **Refinery air/water permitting** and process-safety regulation (PSM/RMP).
- **Anti-gouging / pricing regulation** in our retail markets — the reason we pass
  crude spikes through on a lag rather than spiking the pump the day of a headline.

## Maritime & trade

- **Jones Act** considerations on domestic waterborne movements.
- **War-risk** and charter-party clauses on inbound VLCCs through high-risk lanes.

## Escalation rule

Any **material sanctions exposure** — a cargo that might touch Iranian crude, a newly
designated counterparty or vessel in our book — escalates to the principal the **same
day**, alongside refinery safety/environmental signals and unhedged exposure above the
desk limit.
