# Halcyon Motors — Operations & Supply Chain

Owner: **Chris Donovan (Head of Operations)**

## The ramp
The defining operational goal this year is lifting C1 production from ~**2,600** to
**4,000 units/month** to clear a ~31,000-unit backlog — without shipping quality or
safety debt. The ramp is gated primarily on **battery cell supply** and on quality
holds.

## Battery cell supply
- The C1 uses **LFP** cells. Today they are **single-sourced from CATL**.
- Single-sourcing is both a **supply risk** (one disruption stalls the line) and a
  **policy risk** (tariffs and IRA sourcing rules can penalize Chinese-supplied
  cells).
- **Active initiative:** qualify a **second LFP source** — evaluating **Panasonic**
  and **LG Energy Solution**. This is the top supply-chain risk to retire before the
  ramp.

## Supplier and logistics watch
Operations continuously monitors the inputs that can stall the line:
- **Battery suppliers** — CATL, Panasonic, LG Energy Solution: capacity, pricing,
  plant disruptions, and any quality recalls on shared cell lines.
- **Lithium and raw-material prices** — they flow directly into cell cost and our
  per-vehicle margin.
- **Tariff and trade actions** on imported cells and components.
- **Logistics disruptions** — shipping, port, and inbound-parts risk.

## Quality and recalls
Quality signals and any recall-adjacent issue (ours or on a shared supplier
component) are treated as high-severity. A relevant **NHTSA** action on a shared
part, or a supplier quality escape, escalates immediately — operationally to
Operations and, when safety-related, to the principal same-day.

## Signals Operations watches
- Battery-supplier capacity, pricing, and disruption news.
- Lithium / raw-material price moves.
- Tariff actions affecting imported cells or components.
- NHTSA recalls touching shared suppliers or components.
