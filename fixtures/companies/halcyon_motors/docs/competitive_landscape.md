# Halcyon Motors — Competitive Landscape

Halcyon competes for the **mainstream, value-driven, first-time EV buyer**. Our
read on the field, and what we watch for from each player:

## Tesla (TSLA)
The volume benchmark and the price-cut pace-setter. The **Model Y** is the car our
customer cross-shops most. Tesla's periodic price cuts and financing changes are
the single biggest external force on our reservation funnel. We deliberately do
**not** match every cut — that would destroy our path to gross-margin-positive —
but a Tesla cut *paired with* a tax-credit change is a same-day escalation.
**Watch:** Model Y price changes, new "affordable model" announcements, Supercharger
network policy, FSD/pricing bundles.

## Rivian (RIVN)
Premium adventure brand moving downmarket with the **R2/R3**. Not a direct price
competitor today, but the R3 in particular could land near our segment.
**Watch:** R2/R3 pricing, delivery timelines, capital raises.

## Lucid (LCID)
Luxury range leader with halo technology and limited volume. Not a volume threat,
but a source of battery/efficiency tech signals and talent.
**Watch:** efficiency tech, layoffs/hiring, any move toward a cheaper model.

## BYD
The global cost leader and the **structural long-run threat**. Currently
**tariff-gated out of the US market**, but BYD's cost structure sets the ceiling on
what the whole affordable-EV segment can charge. Any change in US tariff policy
toward Chinese EVs is a strategic-level event for us.
**Watch:** US tariff/trade policy, Mexico-plant plans, global pricing.

## Ford (Mustang Mach-E, F-150 Lightning)
Incumbent with a dealer and service network we lack. The Mach-E overlaps our buyer
on price and body style.
**Watch:** Mach-E incentives, EV strategy shifts, plant/production cuts.

## GM / Chevrolet (Equinox EV, Blazer EV)
Our most **direct mainstream-price competitor**. The Equinox EV explicitly targets
the affordable-crossover buyer we built the C1 for.
**Watch:** Equinox EV pricing and availability, Ultium production ramp, incentives.

## Hyundai / Kia (Ioniq 5, EV6, EV9)
Strong value plus fast charging — arguably the toughest cross-shop for a
well-informed value buyer.
**Watch:** new model-year pricing, charging-speed claims, US production (for credit
eligibility).

## Polestar
Design-led, NACS-forward, modest volume. A reference point for charging and design
positioning more than a volume threat.
