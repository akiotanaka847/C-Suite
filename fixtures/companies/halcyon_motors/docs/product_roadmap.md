# Halcyon Motors — Product Roadmap

Owner: **Jordan Avery (CEO, acting Head of Product & Engineering)**

## Halcyon C1 (shipping)
Affordable electric crossover, single trim, $36,900 starting price.

- **LFP battery**, NACS-native charging port (Supercharger access from day one).
- Software/OTA platform with over-the-air updates.
- Near-term OTA priorities:
  - **Range-estimation accuracy** improvement (currently rolling out to ~60% of the
    fleet) — directly targets the "range honesty" value and reduces buyer anxiety.
  - Charging-session reliability and Supercharger hand-off polish.
  - Service-scheduling and mobile-service experience inside the app.

## Charging & ownership experience
Charging access is the top stated anxiety of our target buyer, so the charging and
ownership experience is treated as **part of the product**, not an afterthought.
NACS-native hardware plus an in-app charging map and transparent cost estimates.

## C2 sedan (decision pending)
A sedan on the next-generation platform. The open decision this year is
**build now vs. defer** until the C1 reaches gross-margin-positive.

- A board decision memo is in progress, led by Product with Finance and Strategy
  input.
- Tension: committing capital to C2 now accelerates the lineup but extends the
  runway risk before C1 margins turn positive.

## Product principles
- **Protect the price point.** Every program trade-off is checked against the
  affordable starting price first.
- **Range honesty over spec-sheet theater.** We publish realistic range and improve
  estimation accuracy rather than chase headline numbers.
- **Don't chase Tesla on acceleration.** Win on total cost of ownership and charging
  access instead.

## Competitive product watch
We maintain teardown comparisons against the **Tesla Model Y**, **Hyundai Ioniq 5**,
and **Chevy Equinox EV**. New competitor model-year changes, charging-speed claims,
or software features should refresh the relevant teardown and, when material, route
a recommendation to Product.
