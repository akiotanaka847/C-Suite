# Halcyon Motors — Financial Snapshot

Owner: **Sam Rivera (Head of Finance)**

## Headline numbers
- **Trailing revenue:** ~$140M (vehicle sales).
- **Monthly burn:** ~$18M.
- **Runway:** ~14 months.
- **Gross margin per vehicle:** ~**-6%** (negative). Reaching breakeven is the
  company's north-star metric this year.
- **Average selling price:** ~$36,900.
- **Vehicles delivered (last quarter):** ~9,800.
- **Order backlog:** ~31,000 units.

## The margin story
Per-vehicle gross margin is negative today, driven by sub-scale production and
bill-of-materials cost. Three levers move it toward breakeven:

1. **Ramp-driven fixed-cost absorption** — getting from 2,600 to 4,000 units/month
   spreads fixed costs across more vehicles.
2. **BOM cost-downs** — chiefly battery cell cost, where a qualified second LFP
   source improves negotiating leverage against CATL.
3. **Pricing discipline** — holding price through Tesla Model Y cuts instead of
   reflexively matching them, which would push breakeven further out.

## Financing posture
We are pre-next-raise. The path to gross-margin-positive *before* the next raise is
what de-risks the round, so anything that delays breakeven (a forced price match, a
cell-supply disruption, a tax-credit loss) is financially material.

## External signals Finance watches
- **Competitor financials and capital raises** (Tesla, Rivian, Lucid) — they shape
  the cost of capital and investor expectations for the sector.
- **Interest-rate moves** — they change both our cost of capital and customer auto-
  loan affordability, which is acute for a value buyer.
- **Tesla price cuts** — a cut we feel forced to match is a direct margin hit; the
  recommendation almost always routes through Finance before any pricing move.
- **EV tax-credit (IRA §30D) changes** — a lost or restored $7,500 credit changes
  the effective price to the customer and our reservation economics overnight.
