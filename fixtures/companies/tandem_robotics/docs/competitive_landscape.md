# Tandem Robotics — Competitive Landscape

Tandem competes for the **mid-market warehouse / 3PL operator** who needs labor on
the floor and cannot afford fixed automation. Our read on the field, and what we
watch for from each player:

## Tesla (Optimus)
The vertically integrated **scale and unit-cost threat**. Optimus is not deploying
into third-party warehouses at scale yet, but Tesla's manufacturing depth means it
could set the long-run cost floor for the whole category.
**Watch:** Optimus production milestones, internal Tesla factory deployments, any
external-customer or pricing announcement, hand/actuator cost claims.

## Figure AI
The best-funded humanoid pure-play and our most direct commercial rival. Figure's
BMW and logistics pilots target the same buyer.
**Watch:** new deployment/customer wins (e.g., GXO-type logistics rollouts), RaaS
or pricing announcements, capital raises, autonomy/throughput claims.

## Agility Robotics (Digit)
**Furthest into real warehouse deployment** (GXO, Amazon pilots) and the closest
cross-shop for an operator evaluating a humanoid today.
**Watch:** Digit deployment counts, RaaS pricing, uptime/throughput data, new 3PL
customers, RoboFab capacity.

## Apptronik (Apollo)
Mercedes and GXO pilots with a NASA pedigree; a credible enterprise-deployment
competitor.
**Watch:** Apollo pilot-to-deployment conversions, manufacturing partnerships,
funding, manipulation capability.

## 1X Technologies
Norway-based, focused more on home and light-commercial than dense warehouse work.
A talent and capability signal more than a direct deployment threat today.
**Watch:** commercial pivots toward logistics, autonomy demos, funding.

## Boston Dynamics (Atlas)
Hyundai-backed R&D leader; the new electric Atlas is a capability bellwether.
Not a volume RaaS competitor yet, but a source of capability and talent signals.
**Watch:** electric Atlas commercialization, Hyundai factory deployments, talent moves.

## Unitree / UBTech
The global **cost leaders** and the structural long-run threat. Currently
**export-control and tariff-gated** out of US enterprise deployments, but their
cost structure sets the ceiling on what the whole RaaS segment can charge. Any
change in US policy toward Chinese robotics is a strategic-level event for us.
**Watch:** US export-control/tariff posture, enterprise/Western expansion, pricing.

## Amazon Robotics
The incumbent buyer building automation **in-house** (Proteus, Sequoia, Digit
pilots). Less a vendor rival than a signal of how fast the largest operators
internalize humanoids — which shapes the mid-market's expectations.
**Watch:** in-house humanoid programs, third-party pilot expansions, vendor choices.
