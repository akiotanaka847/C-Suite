# Tandem Robotics — Operations & Supply Chain

Owner: **Chris Donovan (Head of Operations)**

## The ramp
The defining operational goal this year is growing the billable deployed fleet from
~**380** to **600 robots** and converting the 24-site pilot pipeline — without
shipping quality or safety debt onto a customer floor. The ramp is gated primarily
on **critical-component supply** and on field-uptime holds.

## Critical-component supply
- Tandem robots depend on **actuators, harmonic drives, and rare-earth magnets**.
  Today these are **single-sourced from China**.
- Single-sourcing is both a **supply risk** (one disruption stalls the build) and a
  **policy risk** (export controls and tariffs can penalize Chinese-supplied
  components and magnets).
- **Active initiative:** qualify a **second source** (US/EU) for actuators and
  magnets, paired with US final assembly. This is the top supply-chain risk to
  retire before scaling past 600 robots.

## Supplier and logistics watch
Operations continuously monitors the inputs that can stall the build or a deployment:
- **Component suppliers** — actuators, harmonic drives, rare-earth magnets, and
  GPU/compute: capacity, pricing, plant disruptions, and quality escapes.
- **Rare-earth and raw-material prices** — they flow directly into component cost
  and per-robot margin.
- **Export-control and tariff actions** on imported components and magnets.
- **Logistics disruptions** — shipping, port, and inbound-parts risk.

## Field uptime, quality, and safety
Deployed-fleet uptime is the product. Quality escapes and any safety-adjacent issue
on a shared human floor are treated as high-severity. A safety signal at a deployed
site, or a critical-component quality escape, escalates immediately — operationally
to Operations and, when safety-related, to the principal same-day.

## Signals Operations watches
- Component-supplier capacity, pricing, and disruption news.
- Rare-earth / raw-material price moves.
- Export-control and tariff actions affecting imported components or magnets.
- Workplace-safety incidents or findings touching shared-floor humanoid deployments.
