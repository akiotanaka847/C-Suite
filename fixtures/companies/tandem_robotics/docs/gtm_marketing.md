# Tandem Robotics — GTM & Marketing

Owner: **Morgan Patel (Head of Marketing & People)**

## Positioning
Tandem is the **humanoid that actually works a shift** — the robot a mid-market
3PL deploys to cover the shifts it cannot staff, not a research demo. The brand
voice is operational and proof-driven, never sci-fi.

## The core narrative: cost-per-pick versus labor
Our central marketing asset is a **transparent cost-per-pick story** comparing a
Tandem robot-hour against fully-loaded human labor (wage, turnover, training,
overtime) and against rival RaaS rates from **Figure** and **Agility**. We sell the
math and the uptime data, not the dexterity reel.

## Funnel
- Pilot-led, deploy-to-convert motion (land a paid pilot, prove uptime, expand).
- Current blended customer acquisition cost: ~$11,200 per booked pilot (target:
  under $9,000).
- Pilot-to-deployment conversion: ~22% in one quarter (target: 35%).

## Competitive messaging response
Because rival deployment wins and pricing moves shift our pipeline directly,
Marketing keeps a **rapid-response playbook**:
- A **Figure/Agility deployment or RaaS price move** → publish/refresh the
  cost-per-pick and uptime comparison rather than cut rate; coordinate with Strategy.
- A **competitor autonomy/throughput claim** → counter with our deployed-fleet
  uptime and autonomy-rate data.
- An **export-control or tariff headline** → align messaging on our US-assembly and
  second-source posture so prospects don't read supply risk into a Tandem pilot.

## Signals Marketing watches
- Competitor deployment wins, RaaS pricing, and pilot announcements (Figure,
  Agility, Apptronik).
- Competitor capability demos and throughput/uptime claims.
- Warehouse-labor cost, shortage, and turnover coverage in trade and mainstream press.
- Policy news (export controls, OSHA) that prospects may read as deployment risk.

When a signal warrants a customer-facing response, the recommendation routes to
Marketing; pricing decisions are escalated to Strategy/Finance first.
