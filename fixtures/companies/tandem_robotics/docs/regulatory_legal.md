# Tandem Robotics — Regulatory & Legal

Owner: **Taylor Brooks (Head of Strategy & Customer, also Head of Legal & Regulatory)**

In humanoid robotics, policy moves the deployment calculus as much as capability
does. Three areas dominate Tandem's legal/regulatory watch.

## 1. Export controls and component sourcing
Our actuators, harmonic drives, and **rare-earth magnets** are single-sourced from
China today — which makes sourcing a *legal* risk as well as a supply risk.

- **Watch:** US export-control rulings and entity-list actions touching robotics
  components, Chinese rare-earth/magnet export restrictions, and FEOC-style
  sourcing rules tied to any deployment incentives.
- **Impact:** A control on rare-earth magnets or actuators can strand the fleet
  ramp and raise component cost overnight — directly hitting per-robot margin
  (Finance) and the deployment schedule (Operations).

## 2. Tariffs and trade policy
- **Watch:** US tariffs on Chinese robotics components, motors, and magnets;
  Section 301 actions; any change to the Unitree/UBTech import posture.
- **Impact:** Tariffs on imported components raise our bill of materials directly.
  A change in the Chinese-robotics import posture also moves the long-run RaaS price
  ceiling for the whole segment (the Unitree/UBTech threat).

## 3. Workplace safety on shared floors (OSHA)
- **Watch:** OSHA guidance and rulemaking on humans and robots sharing a workspace,
  ANSI/RIA robot-safety standards, and any incident-driven enforcement at peer
  deployments.
- **Impact:** Safety signals are high-severity by default. A workplace-safety
  finding — ours or at a peer on a shared-floor humanoid — escalates to the
  principal same-day and can gate new deployments.

## Escalation posture
Legal/Regulatory operates at **escalate** authority: material legal or regulatory
risk reaches the principal (Jordan) the same day rather than being auto-actioned.
Contract matters (RaaS service agreements, SLAs, supplier and financing contracts)
and compliance findings route through Legal for review before any commitment.
