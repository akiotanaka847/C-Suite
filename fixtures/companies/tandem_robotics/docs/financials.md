# Tandem Robotics — Financial Snapshot

Owner: **Sam Rivera (Head of Finance)**

## Headline numbers
- **Recurring revenue (ARR):** ~$90M (RaaS subscriptions on deployed robots).
- **Monthly burn:** ~$14M.
- **Runway:** ~16 months.
- **Gross margin per deployed robot:** ~**-9%** (negative). Reaching breakeven is
  the company's north-star metric this year.
- **Average revenue per robot-month:** ~$4,500.
- **Robots deployed (billable):** ~380.
- **Pilot pipeline:** ~24 sites to convert.

## The margin story
Per-robot gross margin is negative today, driven by sub-scale fleet manufacturing
and a high cost-to-serve from teleop interventions. Three levers move it toward
breakeven:

1. **Autonomy-driven cost-to-serve reduction** — raising the autonomy rate from
   82% toward 95% cuts the teleop labor that erodes per-robot-hour margin.
2. **Component cost-downs** — chiefly actuators and rare-earth magnets, where a
   qualified second source improves negotiating leverage and de-risks single-source
   China supply.
3. **Pricing discipline** — holding the RaaS rate through rival cuts (Figure,
   Agility) instead of reflexively matching them, which would push breakeven out.

## Financing posture
We are pre-next-raise. The path to gross-margin-positive *before* the next raise is
what de-risks the round, so anything that delays breakeven (a forced RaaS price
match, a component-supply disruption, an export-control hit on magnets) is
financially material.

## External signals Finance watches
- **Competitor financials and capital raises** (Figure, Agility, Apptronik) — they
  shape the cost of capital and investor expectations for the sector.
- **Interest-rate moves** — they change our cost of capital and the customer's
  build-vs-buy math against capex automation.
- **Rival RaaS pricing** — a rate we feel forced to match is a direct margin hit;
  the recommendation almost always routes through Finance before any pricing move.
- **Export-control / tariff changes** on China-sourced actuators and rare-earth
  magnets — they raise our component cost and per-robot margin overnight.
